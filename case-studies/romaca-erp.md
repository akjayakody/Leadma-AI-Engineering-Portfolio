# Case Study: Romaca ERP

## Overview
Romaca ERP is a working prototype for embroidery manufacturing operations. Its documented process spans sample development, costing, order management, receiving, production, quality control, finished goods, dispatch and invoicing.

## Business problem
Manufacturing operations need connected information across commercial, production, inventory, finance, HR and quality processes. Disconnected records can lead to inaccurate costing, stock discrepancies, weak approval controls and limited operational visibility.

## Capabilities documented
- Customer onboarding and approval.
- Sample and design stages with revision history.
- Server-calculated costing and controlled quotations.
- Sales orders tied to accepted quotations.
- Machine-capacity planning and production release controls.
- Inventory, purchasing, goods receiving and supplier invoice three-way matching.
- Double-entry financial ledger and management reports.
- Attendance, payroll and maintenance workflows.
- Quality sampling, finished-goods labelling and dispatch scanning.

## Technology
React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind, Express, PostgreSQL 17, Prisma 7, Firebase Authentication emulator and Docker.

## Verification and limitations
The README identifies the application as prototype v0.6 and documents 67 automated tests. It also states that production Firebase projects and production environment configuration are still required before going live. The README lists additional payroll/statutory and device-integration items that are not yet included.

## Portfolio relevance
This project demonstrates applied enterprise workflow engineering, transaction controls, approval routing, operational data modelling and cross-functional ERP design.
